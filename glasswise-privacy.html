GlassWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · WildTerra Labs LLC, Washington, USA

The short version: GlassWise keeps your data on your device. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app doesn't have internet access, so it can't send your data to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

GlassWise is made by WildTerra Labs LLC, based in Washington State, USA. Questions about this policy: wildterralabsllc@gmail.com.

What GlassWise stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device (and on your watch, if you use the watch app). We never receive it.

  • Drinks you log (the amount, the time, and where you logged it from: the app, a notification, a widget, the Quick Settings tile or your watch), your daily goals and your history.
  • Your settings: units, containers, wake and sleep times, reminder, nagging and personality choices, and the optional weight and activity level used to suggest a goal.
  • Your sex (optional: male, female or rather not say). It is used only to suggest a starting daily goal when you haven't given a weight. It isn't used for anything else, and it isn't sent to your watch.
  • Reminder state, such as snooze counts and which personality lines you've already seen.
  • While walking nudges are on (they are on for a new install, and you can switch them off in Settings): the step counter's reading next to each drink you log, the time of the last nudge and how many were sent today.

The personality lines are written in advance and built into the app. No AI service is involved, and nothing you log is sent anywhere to produce them.

Permissions and why GlassWise asks for them

  • Notifications: to show reminders, confirmations and the progress notification (which you can switch off in Settings).
  • Alarms & reminders (optional): to deliver reminders at the exact minute.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: for the reminder buzz.
  • Physical activity (optional): for walking nudges. GlassWise asks at the end of setup, after saying why, and you can refuse, or switch the nudges off in Settings. GlassWise reads your phone's step counter, and uses Google Play services on your device to learn when a walk ends, so it can count steps since your last drink. Step data stays on your device.
  • A few other permissions are added by Android libraries the app uses to keep scheduled work reliable, such as keeping the phone awake for a moment or checking network state. None of them gives GlassWise internet access.

Wear OS watch app

If you install the watch app, your drinks and today's progress sync between your phone and your watch through Google Play services. When the two aren't connected by Bluetooth, Google may relay this data through its servers, end-to-end encrypted between your phone and your watch. WildTerra Labs never receives it. Google's handling of data in Google Play services is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

  • What is synced: a drink you log on the watch (its amount and time, and a random id so it is never counted twice), an undo, and the phone's summary of today: today's total, your goal, your unit, your quick-add amounts with their names (including any name you gave a container yourself) and the time your day starts. Nothing else.
  • What the watch keeps: the latest summary from the phone, and any drinks it has not yet handed to the phone. The watch app has no account, no sign-in and no internet permission of its own.
  • Only your own devices: only the GlassWise apps on your phone and watch can read it.
  • If you don't install the watch app, none of this happens.

Backups and exports

  • Android backup: if you've turned on backup for your Google account, Android may include GlassWise's data in your device backup, stored in your Google account in encrypted form. This is controlled by your phone's settings, not by GlassWise.
  • Exports: exporting creates a CSV or JSON file that you choose where to send. The JSON file also records your sex, if you chose male or female. Once you share it, that copy is handled by whoever or whatever receives it.
  • Imports: importing a backup uses Android's file picker. GlassWise reads only the one file you choose.

Health information

GlassWise is a hydration tracker, not a medical device. It gives general guidance only. If you've been told to limit your fluids, follow your clinician's advice.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • We don't show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't require an account.

Children

GlassWise isn't directed to children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal data from anyone.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete everything by clearing GlassWise's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Turn off Android backup, or revoke any permission, in your phone's settings.

Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing to request from us, but you're welcome to contact us with questions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
