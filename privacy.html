Our Commitment to Your Privacy

WildTerra Labs LLC ("we", "our", or "us") develops mobile applications for professional contractors and field technicians. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our apps work.

None of our apps collect, transmit, or share your personal data with us or any third party. All data you enter stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by WildTerra Labs LLC, including:

  • IrrigationQuant Professional irrigation system designer and estimator
  • NodeFlow Irrigation controller and property management

Information We Collect

Information You Enter Directly

Our apps allow you to enter professional and project information for your own use, including:

  • Company name, contact information, and logo (entered in app settings)
  • Customer names and addresses (for quote and project management)
  • Project measurements, notes, and field data
  • Site photos taken or selected from your device gallery

This information is stored locally on your device only using Android's SharedPreferences and local file storage. It is never transmitted to WildTerra Labs or any third party.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not require you to create an account or log in
  • We do not collect location data or GPS coordinates
  • We do not collect usage analytics or crash reports
  • We do not use advertising networks or tracking pixels
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, or messages

App Permissions

Our apps request only the permissions necessary to provide their core functionality.

Photo Library / Media Access

IrrigationQuant NodeFlow

We request access to your photo library to allow you to upload your company logo and attach site photos to projects and quotes. Photos you select are stored locally on your device and are only used within the app. We do not upload your photos anywhere.

Storage / File Access

IrrigationQuant

We request file storage access to save PDF quote files to your device so you can share them with clients. PDF files are generated entirely on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Internet Access

IrrigationQuant NodeFlow

Our apps declare internet permission to enable PDF sharing via your device's native share sheet (email, messaging apps, etc.). Our apps do not connect to any WildTerra Labs servers and do not send any data over the internet.

Data Storage and Security

All data entered into our apps is stored locally on your device using Android's built-in secure storage systems. This includes:

  • App preferences and settings stored in Android SharedPreferences
  • Project data stored in local device storage
  • Photos stored in your device's file system

When you uninstall any of our apps, all locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

We do not operate servers that store your data. We have no ability to access, view, or recover data stored in our apps.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.

Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services including but not limited to:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK or Meta Pixel
  • Crashlytics or other crash reporting services
  • Any advertising networks

The only sharing that occurs is when you explicitly choose to share a PDF quote using your device's native share function — this is entirely under your control and goes directly to the app you choose (email, messaging, etc.).

Children's Privacy

Our apps are designed for professional use by adults and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Our apps are rated for professional/business use on the Google Play Store.

Your Rights and Choices

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information at all times.

  • Access: You can view all your data directly within the app at any time
  • Delete: You can delete individual projects, customers, or entries within the app, or uninstall the app to delete all data
  • Export: You can export your data as PDF quotes or CSV files at any time
  • Portability: Your data belongs to you — export it whenever you need it

For users in the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA), because we do not collect or process your personal data on our servers, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete on our end. All your data rights are exercised directly on your device.

Washington State Privacy

WildTerra Labs LLC is based in Washington State. Washington's My Health MY Data Act and other state privacy regulations are respected in our app design. Our apps are designed with privacy by default — we do not collect personal data, we do not process sensitive data, and we do not engage in targeted advertising.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our apps after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes we will provide notice within the app.
